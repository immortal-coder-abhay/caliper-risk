RESEARCH NOTES

What these results mean.

A single historical cohort

The model uses credit limits, borrower attributes and six months of payment history from the UCI Taiwan dataset. Holdout metrics describe this cohort.

A cost-based decision

The threshold balances missed defaults against declined repaying borrowers. It was selected on the evaluation cohort, so these policy results are retrospective.

Monitoring after training

Population stability index compares batch features with the training reference. A drift signal calls for investigation; it does not measure prediction accuracy.
